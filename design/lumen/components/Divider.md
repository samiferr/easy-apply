A thin rule that separates content.

Use `Divider` to separate groups of content. It is decorative: it never carries meaning on its own.

## Props

`size` — `s` (1px), `m` (2px), `l` (4px, drawn in `content-default`). `orientation` — `horizontal` (default), `vertical`.

## Do

- Use `s` between list rows and within panels, `m` between sections, `l` to close a major region.
- Prefer spacing; add a divider only when spacing alone is not clear.

## Don't

- Don't use a divider to say "this item is selected"; use a fill or border that meets 3:1.
