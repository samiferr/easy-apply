Pill-shaped label with an icon, for counts and states on an item.

Use `Badge` to tag an item with a short state. Filled pill, 12px bold text, with a matching icon for every non-neutral variant.

## Props

`variant` — `neutral`, `informative`, `positive`, `notice`, `negative`. `children` — one or two words.

## Do

- Keep the label under 12 characters.
- Rely on the built-in icon and word, not hue alone.

## Don't

- Don't make a badge clickable; use a button or tag.
